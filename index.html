          How to play

          • Choose a country. Nine rivals are drawn at random. Starting soldiers depend on population.
          • From round 2 you receive 2 soldiers for every country you control and place them as you like.
          • Attack once per turn. You need 2 or more soldiers in the attacking country and always leave 1 behind.
          • Each side rolls one die per fighting soldier, paired in order. The higher die wins a soldier, ties change nothing.
          • Keep rolling until the country falls, you are down to 1 soldier, or you stop. Stopping ends your turn.
          • Conquering a country gains you Max(2, half its soldiers). Then choose how many more soldiers to move in.
          • You can move soldiers between your own countries when no attack is running, always leaving 1.
          • Lose every country and you are out. Conquer all ten nations to win.
          • Anyone with the game link can watch live, but only the signed-in owner can play.

          How your game works

          1. The rivals move first. Turn order is random, so you may watch a few rival turns before yours. Their moves and reasons appear in the game log. Nothing is needed from you yet.
          2. When it is your turn, a "Your turn" banner appears and the buttons under the battle scene become active. From round 2, place your new soldiers first.
          3. To attack: tap one of your countries, then an enemy country, press Attack, then press "Roll the dice!". Keep rolling or stop.
          4. Prefer not to watch the rivals play ? Press "Skip ahead to my turn" at any time.
